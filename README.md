# M39029 Contact ID

**Open the tool: https://chach868.github.io/m39029-contact-id/**

A free quick reference for identifying M39029 crimp contacts by their color bands, with the matching DMC crimp, insertion and removal tooling. It runs in your phone's browser, installs to your home screen, and works offline.

## What it does

- **Color bands to part number:** Tap the three band colors, starting at the wire barrel end, and get the M39029 part number, pin or socket, contact size, wire range and connector series.
- **Tooling:** Crimp frame (with M22520 number), turret or positioner, plus insertion and removal tools.
- **Camera scan:** Take a photo, zoom in and tap each band to read its color. Put the contact on white paper and use "Set white" for better reads.
- **Search:** Look up by M39029 number, BIN code or superseded MS number (e.g. `56-348`, `MS27493-20`).

## Install on your phone

- **iPhone:** Open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** Open the link in Chrome and tap Install app (or menu, then Add to Home screen).

Open it in a real browser, not inside another app's built-in viewer, or the camera buttons may not work. Once installed it works with no signal.

There's also a single-file copy, `m39029-contact-id-offline.html`, you can save and open in a browser. It works best on Android; iPhone file previews won't run it.

## Coverage

About 200 common signal and power contacts: MIL-DTL-38999, 26482 Series 1 and 2, 83723, 83733, ARINC 404 and 600, 24308, AS81511/81714, 28748, 28840 and relay sockets. Coax, twinax, shielded and thermocouple contacts are not included. Entries marked "Check DMC" were unclear in the source chart.

Found a wrong entry, a missing contact, or a color the scanner misreads? [Open an issue](https://github.com/chach868/m39029-contact-id/issues/new) (free GitHub account needed).
