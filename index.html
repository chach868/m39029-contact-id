    About and disclaimer

    A free quick reference for identifying M39029 crimp contacts by their color bands and finding the matching DMC crimp, insertion and removal tooling. Built by a working avionics tech for other techs.

    This is not approved maintenance data. Verify the part number and tooling against current Daniels Manufacturing (DMC) data and the positioner or turret data plate, and follow your approved data and crimp procedures. Color reads from the camera can be wrong; confirm the bands by eye.

    Tooling data is compiled from DMC's published M39029 power contact wall chart. Entries marked "Check DMC" were unclear in the source. It covers common signal and power contacts, not coax, twinax, shielded or thermocouple contacts.
